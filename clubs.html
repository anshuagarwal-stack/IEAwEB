Film Club

Meaningful cinema becomes a starting point for listening, interpretation, discussion and reflection.

  • Watch carefully selected films or important sequences.
  • Notice dialogue, performance, visual choices and filmmaking.
  • Use listening and speaking activities before and after viewing.
  • Discuss characters, choices, conflicts and the film’s central questions.
  • Learn to express a response beyond a simple review or rating.

Film Club is not merely a screening. Members prepare, watch attentively and take part in guided English activities that help them understand both the film and their own response to it.

Reading Club

Literature, essays and important books are read slowly enough to build language and understanding together.

  • Read significant literary and reflective texts.
  • Develop vocabulary through context rather than isolated word lists.
  • Practise identifying main ideas, detail, tone and viewpoint.
  • Interpret difficult passages through guided questions.
  • Share responses and listen to different readings of the same text.

The session gives members time to stay with a text, ask questions and articulate what they have understood. Reading becomes an active encounter rather than a race to finish pages.

Awareness Club

Personal, social and philosophical questions become the basis of structured speaking and reflection.

  • Explore questions connected with everyday life and society.
  • Take part in pair work, group tasks, games and open discussion.
  • Practise speaking clearly without relying on memorised answers.
  • Learn to examine assumptions and listen to opposing views.
  • Use English to communicate observations, reasons and questions.

Awareness Club is neither a lecture nor an ordinary social gathering. It is a participatory English-learning space in which thoughtful inquiry and communication support each other.
